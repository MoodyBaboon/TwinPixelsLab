OchiJump is a mobile platformer game. The core gameplay runs on your device. The Game also includes:

- **A game account and cloud save** — your progress is saved to our servers and can be restored on another device once your account is linked to a wallet.

## 8. Buying Hard Currency (SOL / SKR)

### 8.1 How Purchases Work
Where available, you can buy hard currency with **SOL** or **SKR** from your Solana wallet. When you tap a bundle, our server issues a signed price quote; your wallet app then shows you the exact transfer before you approve it. The amount you pay never exceeds the amount the Game showed you: if the price has risen by more than a small margin, the Game asks you to confirm the new amount instead. The payment is made **directly from your wallet** to the OchiJump treasury through our on-chain payment program (Program ID: `ochiMq7xDD9QHa8bgcjfQf6qf4zCRsamTUKicFmsurX` on Solana Mainnet), and is settled by the Solana network, not by us. Once the payment is confirmed, the hard currency is credited to your game account.

Hard-currency purchases are not available in every version of the Game.

### 8.2 All Sales Are Final
**All purchases are final and non-refundable**, except where applicable law requires otherwise. Because payments are settled on the Solana blockchain, they are **technically irreversible**; TwinPixelsLab has no ability to reverse a confirmed transaction or return funds.

### 8.3 Pending and Failed Purchases
If a transaction is rejected or never sent (for example, insufficient SOL or SKR, you declined it in your wallet, or network congestion), nothing is charged and no hard currency is credited. If you approved a payment, the Game keeps checking it, including on the next launch, and credits it once it is confirmed. If a payment appears confirmed on-chain but the hard currency did not arrive, contact us with the transaction signature and we will investigate.

### 8.4 Purchases Made Before 2.0.0
Earlier versions of the Game sold content through our former on-chain store program. That program is closed. Content purchased through it is unlocked for the account of the purchasing wallet when that wallet signs in.
